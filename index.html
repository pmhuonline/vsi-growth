What this page shows
VSI is positioned for strategic, sustainable expansion — scaling from its current roster of 8 facilities to 50 partner facilities within three years (by mid-2029). This page is built from a single growth-strategy paragraph, so it's intentionally focused on the growth and staffing story rather than program content — what VSI actually delivers at each facility isn't detailed in the source material yet.
The shape of the plan
  • Now: 8 facilities, the current live roster
  • Near-term: structural capacity for 15, no new investment needed
  • Next season: 25, with "manageable adjustments" to program support
  • 3-year target: 50 by mid-2029, via steady one-at-a-time recruitment
  • Acceleration Path: a contingent fast-track if a high-volume funded partner appears
Why the pace is deliberate
The "one-at-a-time" model exists specifically to let leadership maintain high-touch relationship management with every facility as the roster grows — speed is being deliberately traded for relationship quality, right up until the Acceleration Path changes the math.

Target — steady growth model
The growth model
Getting from 25 to 50 facilities follows the same "one-at-a-time" recruitment approach used from day one — deliberately paced, not rushed, so leadership can maintain high-touch relationship management with every facility partner as the roster grows.
Why the pace is deliberate
The steady model exists specifically to protect the high-touch relationship management central to how VSI operates today. Scaling faster would mean trading that away — unless the Acceleration Path gets triggered instead.
Beyond 50
Expansion past the 50-facility mark triggers a planned, comprehensive scaling of VSI's broader operational support structure — meaning the organization itself, not just the facility count, would need to grow up a level. What that comprehensive scaling concretely includes isn't detailed in the source material yet.
✦ Open questions

Acceleration Path
The Fast-Track Scenario
Conditional · Triggered by a high-volume funded partner
Contingent — not yet triggered
The trigger
If a healthcare facility network onboards with full funding for high-volume delivery — the source material's example is 20 sessions per week — the standard one-at-a-time pace no longer fits. VSI is structured to pivot quickly when this happens.
The pivot: a Dedicated Programming Coordinator
The plan is to bring on a Dedicated Programming Coordinator to manage day-to-day operations. That frees executive leadership to focus on strategy and partner development instead of getting pulled into daily delivery logistics.
Why this matters
This is the one scenario in the plan where growth pace decouples from the "one-at-a-time" model — a single well-funded, high-volume partner could move faster than years of steady recruitment.
